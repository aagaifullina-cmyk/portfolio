Privacy Notice

This portfolio uses PostHog analytics to understand how visitors interact with the website and case studies and to improve the portfolio experience.

Data collected

Analytics may include:

  • page views and case views;
  • scroll depth;
  • referral and UTM data;
  • basic technical information required for analytics;
  • interactions with access to selected private case studies.

If you enter a company name to access a private case study, the company name may be recorded for access analytics. Unsuccessful access attempts may also be recorded in order to identify missing company aliases.

I do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other directly identifying personal information through analytics.

Session recordings are disabled.

Cookies and tracking

PostHog is configured in cookieless mode. The website does not use analytics cookies or persistent visitor profiles for this purpose.

Purpose and legal basis

The data is processed for the legitimate interest of understanding how the portfolio is used, evaluating the effectiveness of case studies, and improving the website experience.

Data processor and international transfer

Analytics is processed using PostHog.

The current PostHog project is hosted in the United States, so analytics data may be processed outside Serbia and the European Economic Area using the safeguards provided by PostHog for international data transfers.

Retention

Analytics data may be retained for up to 12 months unless it is deleted earlier.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data related to you, or object to its processing.

You may also contact the competent data protection authority if you believe your data protection rights have been violated.
