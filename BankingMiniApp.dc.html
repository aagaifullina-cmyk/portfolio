Context

A consumer Mini App under NDA

I worked on an MVP for a major banking ecosystem. The product and its core mechanics are under NDA, so this public case focuses on my process, role and design approach.

My role

Product Designer — user flows, wireframes, interaction logic, prototypes, visual direction, states and edge cases, handoff and design review.

Team

I worked with the product stakeholders, the development team, and prepared briefs for illustration and motion.

Discovery

Research first, assumptions second

I started with benchmarking and competitor research to understand familiar patterns, entry barriers and opportunities for differentiation.

We also defined several product hypotheses around behaviour, engagement and the core interaction model. Specific findings and hypotheses are available in the full case.

Open full case →

Validation

Test before handoff

I used interactive prototypes to check the main flows before moving into final UI.

Testing helped reveal where users needed more context, clearer feedback or a simpler sequence of actions.

Design & Iteration

From concept to product

I expanded the chosen direction across the MVP, designing key flows, reusable components, states and edge cases.

As the brief evolved, I explored 15+ visual concepts, gradually narrowing the direction while keeping the core logic consistent.

Outcome

An MVP ready to evolve

The result was an MVP with tested core flows, a defined visual direction and a reusable UI system ready for implementation.

The first release was designed as a starting point for further product and visual iterations based on post-launch data.
